HOW PAYMENT WORKS

You pay the freelancer, not us

Agree the price in chat and settle it directly — UPI, bank transfer or cash, on your own terms. FreelanceKar never holds a rupee, so there is nothing to release and nothing to claw back. If a job goes wrong, support mediates and can pause the freelancer's listing.

Common questions

Does FreelanceKar take a cut of my job?

No. Clients pay nothing extra, and freelancers keep 100% of every job — the platform runs on a simple freelancer subscription, not commission.

What if the work is never delivered?

Raise an issue on the order. Support mediates within 24 hours and can pause the freelancer's listing — and since you pay on delivery, your money is still yours.

How are freelancers verified?

Aadhaar and a live selfie before anyone can quote; PAN and bank details before any payout.

Can I hire someone hyperlocal?

Yes — filter by distance or search a locality. Every freelancer lists the radius they'll travel.
